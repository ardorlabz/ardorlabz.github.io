Advisory & consulting projects

Chemicals

A client faced problems with capacity utilisation and couldn't serve customer needs on time. Thorough value-stream analysis and TOC, plus focused kaizen activities, significantly improved capacity utilisation and OTIF.

Coatings

Grade complexity and an ineffective change-over process were major bottlenecks causing high inventory and slow end-to-end flow. We trained teams, implemented SMED and rationalised product value streams.

Financial services

Lean Six Sigma Green Belt training was delivered to the operations team, who went on to run several Six Sigma projects — achieving significant savings, defect mitigation and productivity gains.

Components

Layout, work-cell design and line-balancing improvements drastically cut end-to-end inventories and improved productivity.
